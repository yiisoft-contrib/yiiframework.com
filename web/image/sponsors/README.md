# Sponsor artwork

Official artwork retrieved on 30 October 2026, used unchanged to acknowledge the
matching public Open Collective sponsors. These marks belong to their owners.

- Skillshare light/dark artwork comes from its [brand guidelines](https://partners.skillshare.com/design),
  using the supplied `SK_Primary_logo_TM_blk_grn.svg` and `SK_Primary_logo_TM_white_grn.svg` files.
- Craft CMS light/dark artwork comes from its [brand resources](https://craftcms.com/brand-resources),
  using black/white SVGs from the [official logo archive](https://craftcms.com/assets/craft-cms-logo-assets.zip).
- The HumHub symbol is served by its [official documentation](https://docs.humhub.org/img/logo.svg).
- The Betteridge monogram is its [official website's pinned-tab artwork](https://www.betteridge.com/_ui/responsive/betteridge/favicon/safari-pinned-tab.svg).

The other leading sponsors retain their public names as text. Do not replace
their marks with similarly named organizations, unofficial redrawings, or a
lower-ranked sponsor's logo. Use official light/dark variants or a neutral
background instead of recoloring the artwork.
