# Design rules

- Follow the visual language of the Yii 3 landing page.
- Prefer clean, flat layouts; avoid boxes unless they convey a real boundary.
- Use separators sparingly and only for major structural boundaries, such as the footer; prefer whitespace for ordinary sections and repeated items.
- Keep headings compact, copy readable, and content widths deliberate.
- Use whitespace and hierarchy instead of decoration.
- Keep buttons consistent with Yii 3, including hover, focus, and active states.
- Use restrained tag/label shapes; avoid excessive rounding, bold text, and underlines.
- Provide intentional light and dark styles for every component and asset.
- Keep navigation pill geometry aligned; dropdowns open beneath their trigger.
- Keep the sticky header seamless, subtly translucent, blurred, and minimally tinted.
- Match the Yii 3 footer structure, spacing, separator, icons, and theme behavior.
- Use SVG icons from the established icon set; keep size, color, and alignment consistent.
- Mark external text links, but not linked images or covers.
- Prefer framework routes to hard-coded internal URLs.
- Remove duplicate content, redundant headings, counters, sidebars, and controls.
- Group content logically and keep primary content wider than secondary navigation.
- Make layouts responsive without sacrificing reading order or readability.
- When removing UI, also remove unused partials, styles, scripts, assets, and data fetching.
